About ZICO

A multifaceted pioneer, 지코 (ZICO) is a seminal figure in modern Korean hip-hop and pop. Beginning as an underground rapper, he gained immense popularity as the leader of Block B, pushing boundaries with bold concepts.

His work as a solo artist, and producer defined an era, known for chart-topping hits that blend genre and sharp lyrical wit. He established his own label, KOZ Entertainment.
